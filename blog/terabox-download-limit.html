What Triggers the "Download Quota Exceeded" Warning?

The daily download quota error is triggered by three distinct factors on TeraBox servers:

  • Account Bandwidth Saturation: Free registered accounts that download more than a few gigabytes within a rolling 24-hour window are temporarily flagged for bandwidth conservation.
  • IP Address Rate-Limiting: If multiple users share the same public IP address (such as on university Wi-Fi, corporate networks, or public VPN nodes), the combined traffic will quickly exhaust the free quota assigned to that IP.
  • Hotlink Flood Protection: Shared links that experience viral traffic spikes (e.g. widely shared movie or game links) are temporarily gated by cloud edge nodes to prevent denial-of-service degradation.

When Does the TeraBox Daily Limit Reset?

If you are downloading through the official TeraBox web interface and hit the quota block, the server reset clock operates on Midnight UTC (00:00 UTC), rather than your local time zone. Depending on where you live:

  • United States (EST): Resets at 7:00 PM (or 8:00 PM EDT).
  • United Kingdom (GMT): Resets at 12:00 AM (Midnight).
  • India (IST): Resets at 5:30 AM the following morning.
  • Philippines / Singapore (SGT): Resets at 8:00 AM the following morning.
